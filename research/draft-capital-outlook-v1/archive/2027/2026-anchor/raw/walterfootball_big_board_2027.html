2027 NFL Draft Big Board

The top prospects available for the 2027 NFL Draft.


By Charlie Campbell

Follow Charlie on Twitter @draftcampbell for updates.

Top-5 Prospects:
1.
Jeremiah Smith , WR, Ohio State. Previously: 1 Avg. 1 per 4
09/23/26: Smith has dominated against Ball State, Texas, and Kent State, totaling 21 receptions for 400 yards and three touchdowns.



08/01/26: In 2025, Smith caught 87 passes for 1,243 yards and 12 touchdowns. Smith (6-3, 215) took college football by storm as a freshman, and he dominated for the Buckeyes. In his debut season, Smith caught 76 passes for 1,315 yards and 15 touchdowns. He showed size, speed, athleticism, and natural feel in a tremendous freshman year, helping Ohio State win a National Championship. For a big receiver, Smith has a rare and special ability to use speed, athleticism, and shiftiness to uncover quickly on short and intermediate routes. He reminds this analyst of Julio Jones during his great seasons in Atlanta.


2.
Leonard Moore , CB, Notre Dame. Previously: 2 Avg. 2 per 4
09/23/26: Moore played really well against Wisconsin with five solo tackles, a pass break-up, blanket coverage, and a near interception. He missed Week 2, but returned in Week 3.



08/01/26: Moore dealt with injuries but played really well in 2025. He had 31 tackles with five interceptions and seven passes defended. With Benjamin Morrison hurt, Moore was phenomenal in 2024, helping the Fighting Irish reach the National Championship. He totaled 48 tackles, two interceptions, two forced fumbles, and 11 passes broken up as a freshman. Moore (6-2, 195) has an excellent skill set with size, speed, and athleticism, and is a natural football player.


3.
Dylan Stewart , DE, South Carolina. Previously: 3 Avg. 3 per 4
09/23/26: In 2026, Stewart has one tackle and made his debut in Week 3. Stewart missed the first two games as he has been slow to recover from hip and back injuries sustained in 2025.



08/01/26: In 2025, Stewart had 33 tackles, 4.5 sacks, three forced fumbles, and two passes defended. The 6-foot-5, 245-pound Stewart was superb for the Gamecocks in 2024. As a freshman, he was an immediate impact player, totaling 6.5 sacks with three forced fumbles, one pass batted, and 23 tackles. Stewart has a lot of potential to develop.


4.
Cam Coleman, WR, Texas. Previously: 4 Avg. 4 per 4
09/23/26: Thus far, Coleman has 11 catches for 178 yards and four touchdowns.



08/01/26: Coleman entered the transfer portal and will play with Texas in 2026. In 2025, Coleman had 56 catches for 708 yards and five touchdowns with Auburn. While other freshman receivers got more attention, Coleman (6-3, 197) flashed tremendous ability with size, speed, and athleticism. He caught 37 passes for 598 yards and eight touchdowns in his debut. If Auburn can give Coleman more consistent quarterback play, he could explode as a sophomore or junior.


5.
Colin Simmons , DE, Texas. Previously: 5 Avg. 6.5 per 4
09/23/26: Simmons had four tackles, two sacks, and a fumble recovery versus Texas State.



08/01/26: In 2025, Simmons had 43 tackles, 12 sacks, three forced fumbles, and two passes broken up. In 2024, Simmons had an excellent debut for Texas and showed serious pass-rush ability with nine sacks. He also had three forced fumbles, 48 tackles, two passes defended, and one interception. Simmons (6-3, 245) has upside and could be even better after gaining experience.



Top-10 Prospects:
6.
Charlie Becker , WR, Indiana. Previously: 6 Avg. 6 per 4
09/23/26: Becker has 12 catches for 272 yards and six touchdowns thus far in 2026.



08/01/26: Becker flashed big-play ability with the Hoosiers in 2025 and showed an ability to stretch defenses downfield. In 2025, he had 30 catches for 614 yards with five scores. Becker illustrated some serious speed with his average of 20.5 yards per reception. To go along with speed, Becker has size and length at 6-foot-4, 204 pounds. He is reminiscent of Alec Pierce with the Colts and coming out of Cincinnati.


7.
K.J. Duff , WR, Rutgers. Previously: 7 Avg. 6.5 per 4
09/23/26: Duff has dominated against UMass (9-196-3) and Boston College (8-137-2). He has 24 receptions for 418 yards with six touchdowns.



08/01/26: In 2025, Duff had a good season with 60 receptions for 1,084 yards with seven touchdowns. Duff is a huge receiver with mismatch size, and he showed some big-play ability with an average of 18.1 yards per carry. The 6-foot-5, 225-pounder has a great skill set and immense upside.


8.
Jadan Baugh , RB, Florida. Previously: 8 Avg. 7.8 per 4
09/23/26: Baugh dominated two weak opponents and destroyed Auburn (26-162-3) including a 53-yard touchdown. He averaged 8.5 yards per carry for 458 yards with eight touchdowns.



08/01/26: In 2025, Baugh averaged 5.3 yards per carry for 1,170 yards with eight touchdowns. He has 33 receptions for 210 yards and two touchdowns. The 6-foot, 228-pound Baugh flashed serious running ability for the Gators in 2024 as a freshman. While other backs got more carries, Baugh showed a dangerous burst and power to get yards after contact. Baugh averaged 5.1 yards per carry for 673 yards and seven touchdowns. He had four catches for 33 yards and a score. Baugh could explode if the Gators make him their feature back.


9.
Trevor Goosby , OT, Texas. Previously: 9 Avg. 9 per 4
09/23/26: Goosby played well against Ohio State in Week 2.



08/01/26: Goosby (6-7, 325) took over as the starting left tackle for Texas in 2025 and had an impressive debut for the Longhorns. Goosby has good size, athleticism, strength, quickness, and upside to get better as he gains experience.


10.
Arch Manning , QB, Texas. Previously: 10 Avg. 10 per 4
09/23/26: Manning has completed 62 percent of his passes for 664 yards with seven touchdowns and two interceptions. After struggling in the first half, Manning led an epic fourth-quarter comeback for a legendary win against Ohio State.



08/01/26: In 2025, Manning completed 61 percent of his passes for 3,163 yards with 26 touchdowns and seven interceptions. He had 10 rushing touchdowns. Manning flashed his excellent skill set in relief work for Quinn Ewers in 2024. Manning has completed 68 percent of his passes for 939 yards with nine touchdowns and two interceptions. He also has two rushing touchdowns. The 6-foot-4, 225-pounder has a strong arm and is athletic with running ability for the NFL. In terms of skill set, style of play, and demeanor, Manning reminds me of Trevor Lawrence.


Top-15 Prospects:
11.
Kewan Lacy , RB, Ole Miss. Previously: 11 Avg. 10.3 per 4
09/23/26: Lacy has averaged 4.9 yards per carry for 191 yards and four touchdowns. He has seven catches for 16 yards. Lacy was injured against LSU.



08/01/26: Lacy (5-10, 205) was a tough runner for the Rebels in 2025. He showed dangerous quickness and shifty running ability while helping the Rebels make a run in the playoffs. Lacy averaged 5.1 yards per carry for 1,567 yards with 24 touchdowns. He also caught 29 passes for 177 yards.


12.
Trey’Dez Green , TE, LSU. Previously: 12 Avg. 12 per 3
09/23/26: Green has 14 receptions for 222 yards and a touchdown.



08/01/26: Green flashed dynamic receiving ability as a sophomore, hauling in 33 passes for 433 yards with seven touchdowns. The 6-foot-6, 238-pound Green has surprising speed and fluid athleticism for a big-bodied tight end. He could be a mismatch weapon in the NFL. As a freshman backing up Mason Taylor, Green caught four touchdowns with 101 yards on 13 catches.


13.
C.J. Carr , QB, Notre Dame. Previously: 14 Avg. 14.5 per 4
09/23/26: In 2026, Carr has completed 73 percent of his passes for 726 yards with six touchdowns and zero interceptions.



08/01/26: Carr (6-3, 205) developed nicely in his first season as a starter, and by the end of the year, he was showing NFL starting ability. Carr completed 67 percent of his passes for 2,741 yards with 24 touchdowns and six interceptions. He was accurate, managed the game well, has developed field vision, and is a dangerous pocket passer with functional mobility. Carr is also known to have great leadership, character, and loves football. Some team sources have told me that they comp Carr to Brock Purdy.


14.
Yhonzae Pierre , DE, Alabama. Previously: 13 Avg. 13.3 per 4
09/23/26: Pierre has nine tackles, one sack, and an interception returned 27 yards for a touchdown.



08/01/26: The 6-foot-3, 248-pound Pierre has been a tough defender for the Crimson Tide in 2025. He has been a dangerous edge rusher with speed and physicality to push through blocks. In 2025, Pierre had 52 tackles with eight sacks, three forced fumbles, and one pass batted. He is an ascending player with a ton of upside.


15.
Dante Moore , QB, Oregon. Previously: 15 Avg. 14.5 per 4
09/23/26: Moore has completed 69 percent of his passes for 849 yards with nine touchdowns.



08/01/26: Moore has been a dynamic point-producer for the Ducks in 2025, making plays with his arm and legs. In 2025, Moore has completed 73 percent of his passes for 3,280 yards with 28 touchdowns and nine interceptions. Moore (6-3, 203) is a dangerous quarterback who is tough to defend. He has shown impressive arm talent and feel as a passer. Moore throws some beautiful deep balls and shows impressive composure and poise for a young player.


Top-20 Prospects:
16.
Tae Johnson , S, Notre Dame. Previously: 16 Avg. 18.3 per 4
09/23/26: In the season opener, Johnson got injured against Wisconsin and left the game early. He missed Week 2.



08/01/26: Johnson (6-1, 197) had a breakout season in 2025 for the Fighting Irish. Johnson was a playmaker showing dynamic ball skills and instincts. He totaled 48 tackles with four interceptions and three passes defended. Johnson was just scratching the surface, and he could become a dynamic safety prospect. Sources say Johnson has worked really hard and is a good character presence for his team.


17.
Jelani McDonald , S, Texas. Previously: 17 Avg. 16.5 per 4
09/23/26: Johnson has 18 tackles thus far in 2026.



08/01/26: McDonald had a strong 2025 season for the Longhorns, showing an ability to make plays as a run defender and in pass coverage. He totaled 80 tackles with three interceptions and three passes defended. McDonald (6-2, 195) has good size and is fast.


18.
Will Echoles , DT, Ole Miss. Previously: 18 Avg. 18 per 4
09/23/26: Echoles has nine tackles and a sack. Against Louisville, he did a nice job of helping to shut down their rushing offense.



08/01/26: Echoles was a disruptive force for the Rebels in 2025 and made a lot of big plays for them at the point of attack. The 6-foot-3, 310-pounder showed run defense and pass rush ability as he totaled 68 tackles with five sacks and five passes batted. Echoles has quickness, athleticism, and strength.


19.
Ashton Hampton , CB, Clemson. Previously: 19 Avg. 19 per 4
09/23/26: On the second play of the blowout loss to LSU, Hampton had a nice interception of Sam Leavitt. He has seven tackles, an interception, and four passes defended thus far.



08/01/26: Hampton (6-3, 204) is a big cover corner that is long and physical. In 2025, he totaled 45 tackles with eight passes broken up and an interception. He had 27 tackles with two interceptions and five breakups as a freshman. His speed and ability to run with speed receivers are a point of emphasis for his evaluation as a junior.


20.
Darian Mensah , QB, Miami. Previously: 22 Avg. 21 per 2
09/23/26: Mensah has completed 90 percent of his passes for 873 yards with 11 touchdowns and zero interceptions.


21.
Vicari Swain , CB, South Carolina. Previously: 21 Avg. 21 per 4
09/23/26: Swain has one tackle thus far.



08/01/26: South Carolina has had a number of good cornerbacks come through the program in recent years, and Swain will keep that tradition alive in 2027. In 2025, Swain totaled 28 tackles with one sack, two interceptions, and five passes broken up. Swain has a good skill set with size, speed, and athleticism.


22.
Ryan Coleman-Williams , WR, Alabama. Previously: 20 Avg. 22 per 4
09/23/26: Coleman-Williams has caught 12 passes for 235 yards and one touchdown.


23.
K.J. Bolden , S, Georgia. Previously: 23 Avg. 21.5 per 4
09/23/26: Bolden has seven tackles.



08/01/26: In 2025, Bolden had 76 tackles with two interceptions and five passes broken up. In a talented secondary, Bolden (6-0, 185) was able to earn playing time as a freshman and had an excellent debut next to Malaki Starks. Bolden totaled 59 tackles with one interception, one pass broken up, one sack, and one forced fumble. After gaining some experience, Bolden could be even better. He could stand to add some weight to his frame for tackling in the NFL.


24.
Ellis Robinson, CB, Georgia. Previously: 24 Avg. 24 per 4
09/23/26: Robinson has six tackles with two passes defended.



08/01/26: In 2025, Robinson had a breakout season for the Bulldogs, playing as an effective cover corner. Robinson totaled four interceptions, seven passes broken up, and 20 tackles. Robinson (6-0, 180) could stand to fill out his frame to help him battle pro receivers.


25.
Will Heldt, TE, Clemson. Previously: 25 Avg. 24.5 per 4
09/23/26: I Heldt has 10 tackles and four sacks. After an underwhelming opener against LSU, he played well against North Carolina.



08/01/26: In 2025, Heldt had 47 tackles with 7.5 sacks and one forced fumble. Heldt has played well for Clemson, showing speed and athleticism off the edge. Heldt played well for Purdue in 2024, recording 56 tackles with five sacks. The 6-foot-6, 260-pounder has good size, a surprising burst, and plays really hard. Heldt has upside, and he could be a nice value pick with upside.
