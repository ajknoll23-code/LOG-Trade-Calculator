**Status:** `frozen_v6p1_pre_activation_feasibility_amendment`

## Confirmation rule

- C2 and C1 remain completely frozen.
- The 504-trade prospective catalog remains completely frozen.
- Primary pass requires at least **0.005** overall equal-cell macro log-loss improvement for C2 versus C1.
- The one-sided **95% upper confidence bound** for overall C2-minus-C1 loss must also be below zero.
- All six composition profiles remain mandatory reported safety diagnostics, but they are no longer six separately powered pass/fail noninferiority tests.

## Power reality check

- At 1,800 votes, estimated primary power if the development effect fully replicated: **100.0%**
- At 2,000 votes, same assumption: **100.0%**
- At 1,800 votes, estimated power if the true effect were only 0.005: **9.1%**
- At 2,000 votes, if true effect were only 0.005: **9.4%**

That distinction is intentional: this feasible study can strongly confirm a material replication of the large development result, but cannot provide high power for an effect that is truly only 0.005.
