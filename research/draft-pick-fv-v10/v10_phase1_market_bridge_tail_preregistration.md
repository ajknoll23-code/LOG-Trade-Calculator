# Draft Pick FV V10 — Phase 1 Market Bridge + Tail Preregistration

Phase 0 froze the fundamental expected-value shape and exposed a nearly uniform absolute scale gap versus KTC. Phase 1 therefore keeps the fundamental estimator unchanged and tests exactly one global market-translation multiplier.
