# Cross-Position Apex Calibration V1 — Phase 1 Preregistration

**Status:** `FROZEN_PRE_EVALUATION`

This is a research-only root-cause study triggered by Brian Burns
currently carrying 7,391 FV while Josh Allen carries 6,564 FV.

It does **not** assume that QB1 must be worth more than DL1. This league
starts both a QB and Superflex while also starting two DL, two LB, two DB,
and two IDP flexes. Cross-position scarcity therefore has to be measured
in the context of the actual league.

Phase 1 changes nothing. It decomposes the current Burns/Allen gap,
measures positional concentration at the top of the live FV distribution,
records the league's positional starter-demand ranges, and calculates only
neutral tie-threshold sensitivities.

A PASS means the >=1.10 Burns/Allen trigger is confirmed and the evidence
is sufficient to design a Phase 2 calibration experiment. It does not
authorize any production change.
