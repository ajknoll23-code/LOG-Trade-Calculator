## Interpretation boundary

Trade Desk comparison holds CURRENT role and production multiplier fixed. Those may differ from the player's true historical role/production state at the birthday; this is a structural age-jump audit, not a causal aging estimate.

The strongest use of this study is to detect implausibly large integer-age discontinuities. It should not be treated as a full causal estimate of aging because real news, injuries, depth-chart changes, draft capital, and production can move market value within the same event window.
